## Defenses
Each time the enemy decides their attack the player is also able to choose a defensive posture, this posture
blocks damage or adds boosts based on the enemy attack chosen. For instance say the enemy chooses a damage
heavy attack with little penetration, if you choose to evade it boosts your agility which blocks more dmg, 
this in turn is a better choice then say a brace which blocks physical damage.
