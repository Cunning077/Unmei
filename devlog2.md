# Week One - The True Start
This week was by far the most enjoyable out of them all, which isnt a high bar bc ive only done 2 weeks. This weeks goals where to start implementing a game loop, one that can be expanded and easily maintanable. While also actually putting real scenes into the game, with it now expanding into 5 different scenes each having multiple actions with state tracking.

## The storyline
The current gameloop follows a charecter who has dementia of sorts and needs lists from his madam to help him through his day, the story also takes place in a medieval time with magic, this is earth but its not at all our earth (how original). The current implentation is the tutorial of sorts and essentially is a walk through of tasks the charecter has to do before going to bed all of which is meant to give insight into who the charecter is building up emotion and tension.

## The art
I use pixel art for each scene and in some cases did use ai generated art (i am not gifted in art). I also used clip art to build scenes. Each piece is rendered using curses in the tui, and represents a single block.
