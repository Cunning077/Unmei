# Attributes
Attributes essentially play a role in battle mechanics each one will play a role in how dmg is calculated on both sides, and in the future will play a role in other world impacts

## strength
Strength is related to you damage, damage scales via your strength
- each point in strength represents 2 dmg
- player starts with 3 points in strength

## agility
Agility is related to you ability to reduce damage or outright dodge an attack
- each point in agility reduces damage by 1% (damage rounds)
- currently at 20 points in agility there is a 10% chance of dodging an attack
- the player starts with 1 point in agility

## intelligence
Intelligence is related to the max mana and mana regeneration of a player
- each point represents 5 mana and 0.5 mana regeneration per second
- the player will start with 0 intelligence

## critical attack
This is the percent an attack will be increased by if a critical attack is activated
- this is represented as an float so 1.10 is %110
- the player starts with this at 0

## critical chance
this represents the chance for the critical attack to actually occur
- a random dice is rolled the decide if this occurs
- this is capped at 100%
- player starts with 0
